# 07 · Design (JACO CORP)

The website uses the JACO CORP design system (claude.ai artifact "JACO CORP"; tokens copied to `design/jacocorp-tokens.json`, brand book in `design/JACOCORP_README.md`).

- **Night** theme (black `#070302`, charcoal `#121619`, one gold `#d8b870`) for the desk, Big Screen and console; **Parchment** (`#f7f1e6`, gold text `#7a5c1e`) for How to Play, matching the printed guide. Players can switch.
- Cinzel for titles, eyebrows and buttons; EB Garamond for everything else, with lining tabular numbers.
- Frames: charcoal fields with a 2px gold outline and 8px corners; no drop shadows.
- The crest (`web/img/crest.png`, taken from the guide with its transparency) appears on dark grounds only.
- Gains and losses add green/red only beside signs (+/−) and arrows; colour is never the only signal.
- Phone first (390×844): a bottom tab bar (Market, Trade, Fund, Results, Score). From 1100px the desk shows two columns. The Big Screen is laid out for 1920×1080.
