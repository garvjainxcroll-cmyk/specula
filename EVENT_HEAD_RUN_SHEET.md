# SPECULA — Event Head run sheet

> **SECRET.** This sheet contains every crisis. Print it for yourself only; never put it on the projector. The console (`/#/admin`) shows the same notes for the current round.

## Before the hall fills

1. Console `/#/admin` open on your laptop (plugged in). Projector on `/#/display`, full screen (F11).
2. Console → **Funds**: one line per team → **Save funds and make codes** → **Print** the codes and hand each team its own code, privately.
3. Teams open `/#/desk` on their phones and join with the code. The Big Screen shows how many members each fund has signed in.
4. While they wait, tell them to read **How to Play** or try **Practice** (two made-up rounds, on their own phone).
5. When every fund has at least one member signed in: **Start the game**. (Funds cannot be changed after this.)

## Results are sealed

- Teams see **only their own fund**: value, profit and loss line by line, Risk Used, forecast marks. There is no leaderboard on the desks or on the Big Screen.
- You see the live standings in the console (Control and Debrief tabs), marked “only you can see this”. Keep the laptop screen away from the hall.
- After Round 06: **Publish the final release** (scores stay sealed), then **Reveal the results**. The Big Screen reveals the standings from last place to first; each desk shows its own score and place.

## Capital, loans and trading notes

- **Starting capital**: console → **Funds** → *Starting capital*, before you press Start. Leave it at ₹1,000 Cr for a fair start.
- **Add or remove capital** at any time except while a round settles: console → **Funds** → *Capital and loans* → amount, a reason (the fund sees it) → **Add** or **Remove**. It is not counted as profit, so it changes a fund’s money, not its score.
- **Loans**: teams ask from their Fund tab; requests appear in console → **Funds** → *Loan requests* (the Funds tab shows a dot). **Approve** (you can change the amount; 8% a year is suggested) or **Decline** with a reply. You can also **Lend** without a request, or **Take repayment**. A loan costs its rate ÷ 4 every round and gives more room under the 3× limit, not more Risk Used.
- **Trading notes** are compulsory: the desk will not send an order without one. Read any fund’s notes in console → **Notes** — useful for the debrief, or to ask a team to explain a trade. Notes do not change scores.

## Every round, the same rhythm (about 13 minutes)

| Step | You press | Teams see | You say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Data drop | (it appears when you press Next) | The new figures, headlines and forecast question | The “say” lines below |
| 2. Window | **Open the window** (10 min; you can choose 1–15) | The clock starts; the desk unlocks | “Ten minutes. Work out the rules.” |
| 3. Crises | **Release now**, whenever you choose — or not at all | A full-screen alert with posts; prices jump | “Check your Risk Used.” |
| 4. The bell | nothing: it settles itself at 0:00 (or **Ring the bell now**) | The desk locks; their own results appear | — |
| 5. Results | read the Results card; then **Next** | Their own profit and loss, rate decisions, why prices moved | Name the biggest moves and why |

**Crisis control is yours.** Every crisis is optional: release any, all or none, in any order, at any moment of the window. Only two rules: a second stage follows its first stage, and two crises follow another (the Concord Rescue follows the Run on the Dinar; the Grand Bargain follows the Reckoning). The round settles with whatever you released. A crisis released in the last 15 seconds gives the desk 30 more seconds.

Controls during the window: **Pause** (orders are refused while paused), **+30 s**, **+2 min**, **Ring the bell now**.

Times below are what the clock shows (it counts down from 10:00). They are suggestions.

## Round 01 — Markets in Normal Times

**Forecast:** Aurelia’s policy rate in the next release.

**Say:**
- Welcome to SPECULA — Round 01 of 06: Markets in Normal Times. Every fund starts with ₹1,000 Cr in cash. There is no leaderboard: you will see only your own fund until the very end.
- The data has dropped. Aurelian inflation is 4.2%, just above its 4% target. Every central bank rule is on your desk — work out what Aurelia will do at the bell.
- Your forecast this round: Aurelia’s policy rate in the next release. When you are ready, open the window: ten minutes on every screen.

**Crisis plan (all optional):** A gentle start. Let the first three or four minutes be quiet so teams learn the desk. Suggested: the Governor hoax at about 6:30, its denial at about 5:00, the crash at about 3:00 — or skip the crash if the room is struggling.

**Crisis — The Governor Hoax** (2 stages)
- Stage 1: *The Governor resigns?* — “BREAKING: the Governor of the Central Bank of Aurelia has resigned, say reports.”
- Stage 2: *It was fake* — “The Central Bank of Aurelia: “The Governor has not resigned. That account is not ours.””
- **Notes:** Stage 1 (“The Governor resigns?”) — let the hall react for 60–90 seconds, then release stage 2 (“It was fake”). If you never release stage 2, the panic stands until the bell: kinder to the hasty, harsher on those who held. Afterwards: “Check your sources — the panic sellers paid the spread twice.”

**Crisis — The Four-Minute Crash** (1 stage)
- Stage 1: *Shares plunge in minutes* — “Aurelian shares plunge 8% in four minutes. No news has been found.”
- **Notes:** Release it with at least two minutes left and say nothing about why. After the bell: “It was a broken trading program. Nothing in the economy changed — those who held their nerve, or bought, were paid.”

After the bell the round ends: its crisis rule changes, trading-cost rises and crisis multiplier end with it.

## Round 02 — Inflation and Interest Rates

**Forecast:** Aurelia’s inflation in the next release.

**Say:**
- Round 02: Inflation and Interest Rates. Energy bills are up everywhere and Aurelian inflation is the highest in a decade: 5.7% in this release.
- Remember: the rules use this release. Higher inflation means higher rates, and higher rates mean lower bond prices.
- When you are ready, open the window: ten minutes on every screen.

**Crisis plan (all optional):** The rate-shock round. Suggested: the Wage Spiral at about 7:00 (it doubles Aurelia’s inflation weight, so Aurelia jumps from 4.25% to 6.25% at the bell instead of 4.50%), then Verana’s bonds crack at about 4:30 and the yield cap at about 2:30.

**Crisis — The Wage Spiral** (1 stage)
- Stage 1: *Strikes and a 9% pay rise* — “Aurelia’s unions win a 9% pay rise; strikes spread to ports and railways. The central bank will now respond twice as hard to inflation.”
- **Notes:** Release with plenty of time left: teams must redo the Aurelia sum. Tell the hall: “Read the rule change on your desk. Do the maths again.”

**Crisis — The Florin Cap** (2 stages)
- Stage 1: *Verana’s bonds crack* — “Verana’s 10-year yield leaps as Aurelian rates soar; Verana’s pension funds sell.”
- Stage 2: *The Bank of Verana caps yields* — “The Bank of Verana will buy unlimited bonds to hold the 10-year yield at 3.00% for the rest of the round.”
- **Notes:** A trap, two stages. Release stage 1 (“Verana’s bonds crack”) and let shorts pile in for two minutes or more; then stage 2 (“The Bank of Verana caps yields”). Say afterwards: “The cap holds Verana’s 10-year yield at 3% — but printing money costs the currency: the florin falls about 6%. In rupees you feel both.” The cap lasts only for this round.

After the bell the round ends: its crisis rule changes, trading-cost rises and crisis multiplier end with it.

## Round 03 — Commodities and Trade

**Forecast:** Calder’s growth in the next release.

**Say:**
- Round 03: Commodities and Trade. Norvik’s oil output has dipped, Aurelian politicians are attacking Calder’s surplus, and Sarvan has had no rain for three months.
- Calder has no oil of its own. Sarvan’s inflation is 8.3%. When you are ready, open the window: ten minutes on every screen.

**Crisis plan (all optional):** Three crises are available — the busiest round. Suggested: the pipeline explosion at about 8:00 and the reroute at about 6:30; the tariffs at about 5:00; the failed harvest at about 3:30 and the bread riots at about 2:00. For a calmer room, choose two of the three.

**Crisis — The Northern Pipeline** (2 stages)
- Stage 1: *Pipeline explosion* — “Explosion on Norvik’s Northern Pipeline: almost half of Norvik’s oil cannot reach the sea.”
- Stage 2: *The Western Line* — “Norvik reroutes oil through the Western Line: two-thirds of lost output back within weeks.”
- **Notes:** Stage 1 (“Pipeline explosion”) — oil jumps. Let funds chase it, then release stage 2 (“The Western Line”) a minute or two later. After the bell: “Calder has no oil of its own — watch its growth next round.”

**Crisis — The Tariff Wall** (1 stage)
- Stage 1: *30% tariffs on Calder* — “Aurelia slaps 30% tariffs on everything Calder makes. Calder widens its currency band to ±6% for the rest of the round.”
- **Notes:** It widens Calder’s currency band to ±6% for the rest of the round and revises Calder’s growth down — tell the hall to read the revision.

**Crisis — Empty Fields** (2 stages)
- Stage 1: *Harvest fails; export ban* — “Sarvan’s harvest fails. The government bans all wheat exports.”
- Stage 2: *Bread riots; emergency hike* — “Bread riots in Sarvan’s capital. The central bank adds 3.00% to its rule in an emergency meeting.”
- **Notes:** Stage 2 (“Bread riots”) adds +3.00% to Sarvan’s rule: Sarvan’s rate goes from 11% to 13% at the bell. After the bell: “A big rate rise can hold up a currency even in a crisis.”

After the bell the round ends: its crisis rule changes, trading-cost rises and crisis multiplier end with it.

## Round 04 — Emerging Markets and Currencies

**Forecast:** Kesh’s inflation in the next release.

**Say:**
- Round 04: Emerging Markets and Currencies. Look hard at Kesh’s reserves: 140 at the start of the game, 61 in this release.
- Kesh’s finance minister says the peg is not up for discussion. Your forecast: Kesh’s inflation. When you are ready, open the window: ten minutes on every screen.

**Crisis plan (all optional):** The biggest crisis of the game. Suggested: the bank queues at about 8:00, the peg breaks at about 6:30 (the dinar falls from 50 to about 73 to the crown), contagion at about 4:30, and the Concord rescue at about 2:30 as a twist. The rescue can only follow the run on the dinar.

**Crisis — Run on the Dinar** (2 stages)
- Stage 1: *Queues at the banks* — “Queues outside Kesh’s banks. The dinar trades at 58 on the street. Kesh adds 3.00% to its rule in an emergency.”
- Stage 2: *The peg breaks* — “Kesh abandons the peg. The dinar floats — and sinks.”
- **Notes:** Stage 1 (“Queues at the banks”) raises the crisis multiplier to 1.6, so many funds will be near 100% Risk Used. Wait at least a minute, then release stage 2 (“The peg breaks”). Say: “Check your Risk Used. Above 100% at the bell means forced liquidation.”

**Crisis — Contagion** (1 stage)
- Stage 1: *Flight from frontier markets* — “Investors flee every frontier market. Sarvan’s ora hits a record low.”
- **Notes:** Release after the peg breaks for the full story; on its own it is a frontier-market scare.

**Crisis — The Concord Rescue** (1 stage) — follows “Queues at the banks”
- Stage 1: *A ₵60 bn rescue* — “The Concord Fund lends Kesh ₵60 bn. Kesh cuts its emergency rate addition to +1.00%.”
- **Notes:** Good news, and a trap for anyone who bought protection at the top. After the bell: “Insurance bought at the top of the panic lost most of its value. Protection pays out only if a default actually happens.”

After the bell the round ends: its crisis rule changes, trading-cost rises and crisis multiplier end with it.

## After Round 06

1. Press **Publish the final release (scores stay sealed)**. The last forecast is marked and every SPECULA Score is calculated. The Big Screen says the results are about to be revealed.
2. When the hall is ready, press **Reveal the results**. The Big Screen shows the standings from last place to first, then the chart of every fund; each desk shows its own score and place.
3. Console → **Debrief**: charts of each economy, every fund’s value, the auction bids against fair value (only you see who bid), and a short “what happened and why” for each round — read these out.
4. Console → **Tools** → **Download backup**.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A team’s phone dies | Another member signs in on their own phone with the same fund code; the desk is shared, nothing is lost. |
| The Wi-Fi drops | Desks show a red “Connection lost” bar and catch up by themselves. Orders already accepted are safe. If it is widespread, **Pause** the clock until it is back. |
| A team says an order “didn’t go through” | Their **This round’s orders** list on the Trade tab shows every order that reached the market. If it is not there, it did not happen. |
| A late order was refused | That is the rule: trades are accepted only while the window is open. |
| A dispute over a score | After the reveal, open the team’s row in the console standings and show the working: every step is there. Before the reveal, the team can check its own numbers on its My score tab. Every price, result and score is calculated for all funds at the same moment; there is no judging and no appeal. |
| The console laptop crashes or the page is reloaded mid-settlement | Open `/#/admin` on any device and sign in. If it shows **Finish settlement**, press it; the result is exactly the same. |
| The projector shows the wrong thing | Reload `/#/display`. It always shows the current state. |
| You pressed Release too early | It cannot be undone; carry on. The crisis effects are the same whenever in the window they happen. |
| You need to restart after a rehearsal | Console → Tools → Reset everything (type RESET). |
