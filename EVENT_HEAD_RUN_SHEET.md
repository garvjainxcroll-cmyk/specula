# SPECULA — Event Head run sheet

> **SECRET.** This sheet contains every crisis. Print it for yourself only; never put it on the projector. The console (`/#/admin`) shows the same notes for the current quarter.

## Before the hall fills

1. Console `/#/admin` open on your laptop (plugged in). Projector on `/#/display`, full screen (F11).
2. Console → **Funds**: one line per team → **Save funds and make codes** → **Print** the codes and hand each team its own code, privately.
3. Teams open `/#/desk` on their phones and join with the code. The Big Screen shows how many members each fund has signed in.
4. While they wait, tell them to read **How to Play** or try **Practice** (two made-up quarters, on their own phone).
5. When every fund has at least one member signed in: **Start the game**. (Funds cannot be changed after this.)

## Every quarter, the same rhythm (about 2½ minutes)

| Step | You press | Teams see | You say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Data drop | (it appears when you press Next) | The new figures, headlines and forecast question | The “say” lines below |
| 2. Window | **Open the window** (2:00) | The clock starts; the desk unlocks | “Two minutes. Work out the rules.” |
| 3. Crisis | **Release now** at the time given below | A full-screen alert with posts; prices jump | “Check your Risk Used.” |
| 4. The bell | nothing: it settles itself at 0:00 (or **Ring the bell now**) | The desk locks; results appear | — |
| 5. Results | read the Results card; then **Next** | Profit and loss, rate decisions, why prices moved, standings | Name the biggest moves and why |

Controls during the window: **Pause** (orders are refused while paused), **+30 s**, **Ring the bell now**. A quarter cannot settle until its crisis has been released; if you release it after the bell, the desk reopens for 30 seconds.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A team’s phone dies | Another member signs in on their own phone with the same fund code; the desk is shared, nothing is lost. |
| The Wi-Fi drops | Desks show a red “Connection lost” bar and catch up by themselves. Orders already accepted are safe. If it is widespread, **Pause** the clock until it is back. |
| A team says an order “didn’t go through” | Their **This quarter’s orders** list on the Trade tab shows every order that reached the market. If it is not there, it did not happen. |
| A late order was refused | That is the rule: trades are accepted only while the window is open. |
| A dispute over a score | Open the team’s score (Score tab → their row) and show the working: every step is there. Every price, result and score is calculated for all funds at the same moment; there is no judging and no appeal. |
| The console laptop crashes or the page is reloaded mid-settlement | Open `/#/admin` on any device and sign in. If it shows **Finish settlement**, press it; the result is exactly the same. |
| The projector shows the wrong thing | Reload `/#/display`. It always shows the current state. |
| You pressed Release too early | It cannot be undone; carry on. The crisis effects are the same whenever in the window they happen. |
| You need to restart after a rehearsal | Console → Tools → Reset everything (type RESET). |
